Ridgeline Trails Site Plan

A Community Hiking & Nature Guide

Site Purpose

To provide a hub for local hikers featuring trail directories, difficulty ratings, live weather data, and interactive maps.

Target Scenarios

  • "What is a trail under 2 miles that is safe for a stroller?"
  • "What is the current wind speed at the trailhead before I leave my house?"
