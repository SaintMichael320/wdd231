NP Membership

Non-Profit · No Annual Fee

Designed for registered South African non-profit organisations. Gain full access to the Chamber's foundational benefits at absolutely no cost.

Member Benefits

  • Chamber membership directory listing
  • Monthly Chamber newsletter
  • Access to general business networking events
  • Member-only webinar access
  • 10% discount on Chamber event tickets
  • Chamber member seal & letterhead rights
Br

Bronze Membership

Emerging Businesses · R750/month

Perfect for new and growing Pretoria businesses ready to build credibility and expand their professional network.

Member Benefits

  • All NP Membership benefits
  • 1 complimentary event ticket per quarter
  • Business profile on the Chamber website
  • 4 hours/month Chamber meeting room access
  • Recognition in the Chamber annual report
  • Access to exclusive member business forums
Ag

Silver Membership

Growing Enterprises · R1,500/month

Elevate your brand in Pretoria's business community. Gain greater visibility, more event access, and the tools to grow with confidence.

Member Benefits

  • All Bronze Membership benefits
  • Quarterly homepage spotlight feature
  • 3 complimentary event tickets per quarter
  • Priority access to Chamber co-working spaces
  • Press release distribution to Chamber network
  • Reduced rates on Chamber publications & advertising
  • Invitation to Silver & Gold member exclusive dinners
Au

Gold Membership

Established Leaders · R3,000/month

The pinnacle of Chamber membership. Maximum exposure, executive access, and personalised support for Pretoria's most established enterprises.

Member Benefits

  • All Silver Membership benefits
  • Monthly premium homepage spotlight feature
  • Unlimited complimentary event tickets
  • Board meeting attendance & advisory voting rights
  • First rights to expo exhibitor space
  • Dedicated Chamber account manager
  • 2 custom training workshops per year
  • Annual Gala Dinner table for four
  • Logo on all Chamber marketing materials
